# Phase 6b: tier 1 coverage study, C3 fixed vs learned Σ scale (`:hier`)

Recorded 2026-11-26 on an Apple M2, Julia 1.12.0. Run with `bench/phase6b_run.sh` as 8
single-threaded processes: n = 2000 took 8.6 h (10:04–18:39) and n = 500 took 2.1 h (18:39–20:43).
All 1600 replications finished, none failed.

**Design:**
- Data: `simulate_selection` in diagnostic mode, scenarios {`:gaussian`, `:mixture`} × n {500, 2000}
  × r2 {0.5, 0.95} × iv = 1.
- Fit: `marginal :common` with prior C3 (fixed Σ scale) or `sigma_scale = :hier` (C3 plus the
  learned scale r, Phase 6a′).
- 100 replications per cell; 4 chains × 6000 iterations, 2000 saved draws per chain, the first 25%
  discarded; `effects()` with `n_g0 = 5`.
- Weak-instrument cells are held for option D (Phase 4g).

**Files:**
- `bench/coverage_tier1_summary.md` (both blocks, generated by `bench/coverage_summary.jl`) has, per
  cell and estimand:
  - coverage of the population truth with its MC se, and coverage of the sample truth;
  - width, bias and RMSE;
  - median R-hat and the share with R-hat > 1.1;
  - mean k, k(≥5%) and the separated share;
  - posterior r against the OLS data value;
  - seconds per replication;
  - the flags.
- The per-replication rows are in `bench/coverage_tier1_n{500,2000}.shard*.csv`.

**Flags:** `*` marks population coverage below 0.90. `**` marks a C3-vs-`:hier` coverage difference
larger than 2 MC se.

## Findings

1. **C3 fails on mixture data at r2 = 0.95, and `:hier` fixes it.**
   - C3's PATT coverage is 0.48 at n = 2000 and 0.61 at n = 500, with bias +0.13 and RMSE 0.31–0.43.
   - The failure holds among converged replications: coverage 0.40 for the 82 at n = 2000 with
     R-hat < 1.1 (bias +0.20), and 0.19 for the 47 such at n = 500. So it is a property of the
     posterior, not of mixing.
   - Mechanism: C3's fixed Σ scale (0.153) is about 15 times the within-type noise (posterior r1 ≈
     0.010–0.011 under `:hier`). The separated share under C3 is 0.23 at n = 2000 and 0.33 at
     n = 500, against 0.03 under `:hier`. A quarter to a third of the units sit in clusters that are
     nearly all treated or all control, so their counterfactual outcome comes from the base
     measure.
   - The pilot (realistic mode, 10 replications) slightly favoured C3 in this cell. Diagnostic mode
     separates the types and exposes the failure.
2. **Gaussian data, r2 = 0.95.**
   - At n = 2000, `:hier` is better on every estimand: coverage 0.96 / 0.91 / 0.94 against 0.93 /
     0.87 / 0.92, RMSE at 0.54–0.69 of C3's, and narrower PATE intervals (0.083 against 0.107).
   - At n = 500, coverage is equal (0.94 / 0.89 / 0.92 against 0.93 / 0.88 / 0.90), but `:hier` has
     heavier tails: RMSE 1.15–1.55× C3's. The median error is close (PATT |err| 0.016 against
     0.014), but a few replications pick up extra clusters (k up to 2.45, one PATT interval of width
     1.2).
   - `:hier`'s time per replication is also 2× there (median 129 s against 57 s). The posterior r
     (0.041, 0.022) is about twice the OLS value (0.019, 0.010): at n = 500 the Gamma(2, 13) prior,
     with mean 0.153, still pulls r up.
3. **r2 = 0.5:** the priors are equivalent, within 0.03 coverage and 0.92–1.09 RMSE ratios, except
   mixture n = 2000, where `:hier`'s RMSE is 0.80 / 0.62 of C3's for PATE / PATT.
4. **The one flag that favours C3 doesn't hold up.** At mixture n = 500 r2 = 0.95, PATE coverage is
   0.98 under C3 against 0.89 under `:hier`. C3's intervals there are 1.8× as wide (1.07 against
   0.59) and 62% of its replications have R-hat > 1.1, so the pooled intervals are inflated by
   chains that disagree. `:hier` has the lower PATE RMSE (0.82×). The `:hier` PATE bias is −0.063
   (0.37 of the interval half-width), which is part of finding 5.
5. **A shared, prior-independent PATT under-coverage on mixture data at small n.**
   - At n = 500, PATT bias is about −0.21 under both priors at r2 = 0.5 (coverage 0.72 / 0.83) and
     −0.16 under `:hier` at r2 = 0.95 (coverage 0.85).
   - It shrinks to about −0.06 at n = 2000 (coverage 0.89–0.93), so it looks like finite-sample
     shrinkage of the type-specific gains, not inconsistency.
   - Likely candidates are the hierarchical intercepts, the β^c prior, and a small treated type
     being merged. It is not diagnosed here and needs its own look.
6. **Mixing on mixture data is poor even at iv = 1.**
   - PATE and PATU R-hat exceeds 1.1 in 42–93% of replications at n = 2000 (worst for C3 at
     r2 = 0.95: 88–93%; 42–72% for `:hier`), and in 23–62% of the r2 = 0.95 replications at n = 500.
   - PATT mixes well (0–6% at r2 = 0.5).
   - Coverage near 1.00 in those cells is partly that inflation. Option D (Phase 4g) should be
     tested on these mixture cells as well as on the weak-instrument ones.
7. **Clusters:** k(≥5%) is 1.00–1.06 on Gaussian data at r2 = 0.95, 1.30–1.84 at r2 = 0.5 (more at
   n = 500), and 3.0–3.25 on mixture data (true 3). Learned r adds a few tiny clusters (mean k up by
   0.1–1.0), but k(≥5%) changes by at most +0.25.
8. **Cost per replication** (mean, with 8 concurrent processes):
   - n = 2000: 248–316 s for C3 and 268–407 s for `:hier` (`:hier` +20–29% on mixture data);
   - n = 500: 63–69 s for both, except `:hier` on Gaussian data at r2 = 0.95 (123 s against 67 s;
     medians 129 s against 57 s).

## Recommendation

**Make `sigma_scale = :hier` the default.** The fixed C3 scale causes the one severe, structural
failure in tier 1: PATT coverage of 0.48–0.61 on mixture data with strong covariates, which holds
in converged chains and is driven by separated clusters. `:hier` removes it, matches or beats C3
on coverage almost everywhere, and has the lower RMSE in 16 of the 24 cell-estimands.

**Costs of switching:**
- heavier error tails and 2× runtime at n = 500 on Gaussian data at r2 = 0.95;
- up to +29% runtime on mixture data at n = 2000;
- a few more tiny clusters.

**Keep these open, outside the default decision:**
- the shared PATT shrinkage on mixture data at n = 500 (finding 5);
- the mixture-cell mixing, for option D (finding 6);
- whether a weaker r hyperprior (a_r = 1, or a rate set from the data) trims the n = 500 tails.
  Phase 6a′ found the a_r sensitivity small at n = 1000.

The default is unchanged in this phase, as asked; it switches after review.
